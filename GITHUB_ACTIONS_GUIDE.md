# 🚀 Instant 1-Click APK Builder for Galaxy Watch Ultra

This repository is pre-configured with **GitHub Actions**. As soon as these files are placed in a GitHub repo, GitHub automatically compiles the `.apk` on its cloud servers and provides a direct `.apk` download link for your phone!

---

### Step-by-Step (Takes Under 2 Minutes):

1. **Create a Free GitHub Account** (if you don't have one):
   - Go to [github.com](https://github.com) and sign in.

2. **Create a New Repository**:
   - Click the **+** icon in the top-right corner > **New repository**.
   - Name it `somnopulse-watch`, choose **Public**, and click **Create repository**.

3. **Upload These Files**:
   - On the new repository page, click **"uploading an existing file"** (or drag and drop this entire folder).
   - Click **Commit changes**.

4. **Get Your APK**:
   - Click the **Actions** tab at the top of your GitHub repository.
   - You will see the **"Build SomnoPulse Wear OS APK"** workflow running with a yellow circle.
   - Wait about 60–90 seconds until it turns green with a checkmark (**✓**).
   - Click on the completed run, scroll down to **Artifacts**, and tap **SomnoPulse-GalaxyWatchUltra-APK**.
   - This downloads the real **`.apk` file** directly to your phone!

5. **Install on Your Watch**:
   - Open **Wear Installer 2** on your phone.
   - Tap select APK. The file will now appear as an active, selectable `.apk` file!
   - Tap **Install**, and it will load onto your Galaxy Watch Ultra with zero developer headaches.
